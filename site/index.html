The flagship reference design

A 400 W maximum-power-point-tracking converter from a 12-60 V solar panel to a 48 V battery. Each half-bridge of a four-switch buck-boost is replaced by a three-level flying-capacitor leg on 100 V GaN. The switches block half the voltage, the inductor ripple falls up to four-fold, and near a 1:2 ratio the leg behaves as a soft-charged switched-capacitor stage. A bidirectional GaN switch disconnects the panel, and another bypasses conversion when the panel already sits at battery voltage.

The design package includes an annotated schematic, a bill of materials with real part numbers, the loss model as a spreadsheet and notebook, and a circuit simulation that reproduces the efficiency curve. It also has the control state machine in C and an HDL modulator, both labelled reference-only.
