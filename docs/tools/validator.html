Efficiency-curve validator

Write down the efficiency your application needs at each load, as a table or as a formula. The validator sweeps the design across load, marks every point that misses, and tells you which loss term to attack there.

Allowed: numbers, p, + - * / ^, parentheses, sqrt abs exp ln log10 min max pow clamp, pi, e. Evaluated by a small parser, never by eval.
