Sources

Every efficiency figure on this site is cited here with the exact conditions it was measured under and what it includes. Where a commonly quoted number could not be verified, this page says so rather than repeating it.

Corrections to commonly quoted numbers

  • The MDPI Sustainability 2024 hybrid switched-capacitor buck-boost paper's 96 % and 99.2 % are photovoltaic energy-harvest ratios, not converter efficiency.
  • A "96-97 % switched-capacitor buck-boost for PV" could not be verified. The closest verified paper (Liang et al., APEC 2012) reports 92.5 %.
  • The CoBB bidirectional-GaN buck-boost results are from MIT and exclude gate-drive loss.
  • The TI GaN optimizer's 99.5-99.6 % is a pass-through state; its switching peak is 99.0 %.
  • The piezoelectric ">99 %" figure (Boles et al. 2021) appears to be calculated rather than measured.
